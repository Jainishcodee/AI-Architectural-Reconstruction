## Verified facts per tirth (for the boards)

### Girnar (Junagadh, Gujarat)
- Moksha-kalyanak bhoomi of **Bhagwan Neminath** (22nd Tirthankar)
- **The 5th tunk**: Jains hold the charan-paduka there is **Neminath Bhagwan's**;
  it is currently under Dattatreya (Hindu) control — a long-running dispute
- Flashpoint: **1 October 2023** — clash at the Dattatreya shikhar involving Digambar
  yatris; FIR against 200 people; "Girnar hamara hai" sloganeering from the other side
- ⚠️ **Presentation note:** keep this board factual and dignified — "aa tunk aaje aapna
  hathma nathi" — no anti-Hindu framing. The event is public; the message is tirth-bhakti,
  not communal anger. State the claim, the loss, and move to the solution.

### Sammed Shikharji / Parasnath Hill (Jharkhand)
- **20 of 24 Tirthankars attained moksha here** — the holiest Jain tirth
- **Dec 2022:** Jharkhand notification promoted it as a **tourist/eco-tourism spot** →
  nationwide silent protests by lakhs of Jains
- Two munis (Sugyeyasagarji, Samarthsagarji) **gave their lives fasting** in the movement
- **5 Jan 2023:** Centre stayed all tourism and eco-tourism activity on the hill
- Ongoing: despite bans, **meat and alcohol are sold around the pilgrimage site**;
  community demands "place of worship" status
- Board line: "Jya 20 Tirthankar moksha pamya, tya picnic?"

### Ranakpur (Pali, Rajasthan)
- Chaturmukh Dharna Vihar — Adinath Bhagwan, ~1444 carved pillars, 15th century
- ⚠️ Your points ("khandit pratimas", "most land taken by government") — I could
  **not verify these online**. Ranakpur is managed by Anandji Kalyanji Pedhi. Get the
  specifics from whoever briefed you (which pratimas, which land, when) before painting
  the board — a wrong claim here undermines the two rock-solid boards next to it.
  Safe alternative angle if unverified: Ranakpur as "tourist spot vs tirth" — dress
  codes, photography, guides treating a living derasar as a monument.

reveal name + distance + one wow-fact. Candidate list (⚠️ finalize with sangh elders —

## Index poster (outside the door)

### Poster copy — FINAL (v5: question raised, answer asserted)

> # તીર્થ પોકારે છે — જવાબ આપણે છીએ.
> ### દૂરનાં તીર્થ ઝૂરે છે... સાચવવાનો વારો આપણો. ⛰️
> ### નજીકનાં દેરાસર અજાણ્યાં... ઓળખવાનો વારો તમારો. 🛕

- Line 1 = hook + assertion in one: the tirth calls out — **we** are the answer
  (promises the room gives solutions, not just problems)
- Line 2 = the three tirths wrapped in one line, closed assertively — "our turn to protect"
- Line 3 = Hidden Jinalayas quiz, closed assertively — "your turn to discover"
- Lines 2–3 are deliberately parallel (ઝૂરે/અજાણ્યાં... વારો આપણો/વારો તમારો) — reads
  like one breath, and the આપણો→તમારો shift points the finger at the visitor
- Footer small print if space allows: *"પગલાં પડશે યાત્રિકનાં, તો પગલાં રહેશે પ્રભુનાં."*

**Assertive alternates:**
- Title: "તીર્થ કોના ભરોસે? — આપણા જ." (question asked AND answered in 6 words)
- Title: "તીર્થ ઊભાં છે — હવે વારો આપણો."
- Tirth line: "વ્યથા પણ અંદર છે... ને ઉપાય પણ." (asserts the room holds the solution)
- Mandir line: "ઘર પાસેનાં દેરાસર — આજથી નજરમાં."

**How the riddles map (don't print this — this is the reveal inside the room):**
- ઘર આપણું, એક ઓરડાની ચાવી બીજા પાસે → Girnar: the tirth is ours, the 5th tunk isn't
- કોઈ માથું નમાવવા, કોઈ ફોટા લેવા → Shikharji: yatrik vs tourist
- થાંભલા હજાર, ટેકો કોઈ નહિ → Ranakpur: 1444 pillars hold the roof, but who holds
  the tirth? (થાંભલો/ટેકો pun — pillar vs support)
- દૂરનું જોયું, ઘર પાસેનું રહી ગયું → the Hidden Jinalayas quiz
- સમજાયું નહિ ને? અંદર આવો → the CTA; the confusion IS the hook
- પગલાં/પગલાં tagline → footsteps of yatriks vs Prabhu's charan-paduka (Girnar hit)

Every word is everyday-simple (ઘર, ચાવી, ઓરડો, ફોટા, થાંભલા, રમત) — nothing reveals
tirth names or issues. Inside the room, repeat each riddle line as the heading of its
board, so the "ohhh THAT's what it meant" moment lands three times.
