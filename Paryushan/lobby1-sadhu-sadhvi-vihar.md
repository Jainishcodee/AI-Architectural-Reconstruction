## Verified incidents for the narration

### 1. Muni Shri Jambuvijayji Maharaj Saheb — "The Moving University"
- **12 November 2009, 6:55 AM**, Balotra–Barmer road, Rajasthan
- On vihar from **Nakoda toward Jaisalmer** — going to work on the ancient gnan
  bhandars (manuscript libraries) there
- Hit **from behind by a speeding jeep**; his disciple **Muni Namaskarvijayji (age 34)**
  also died on the spot
- Age 87. World-renowned aagam scholar — discovered, catalogued and published texts
  from forgotten gnan bhandars; guru to university scholars across the world; master
  of many languages (Sanskrit, Prakrit, Tibetan, Pali and more)
- ⚠️ The "22 languages / saving tirth from Pakistan" detail — verify with your source
  before putting it on a board; the *documented* fact is he was en route to Jaisalmer
  (border region) for gnan-bhandar preservation work. That loss framing still stands:
  what he knew, nobody alive fully knows now.

### 2. Rewa, Madhya Pradesh — just 3½ months ago
- **20 May 2026, morning** — three Digambar sadhvis on padvihar near the Rewa
  Collectorate, hit by a speeding car
- **Shruti Mati Mata** died on the spot; **Upasham Mati Mata** died soon after;
  Arika Mata critical
- The driver fled **270 km** before being caught
- Use this as the "aa aaj pan chalu chhe" (this is still happening TODAY) moment —
  it happened this very year, weeks before this Paryushan

### 3. Pattern point (for narrator)
- Vihar accidents recur every year on highways — monks walk at dawn/dusk on roads
  with no footpaths, trucks at full speed, low visibility. This is not one tragedy;
  it is a bleeding wound of the shasan.

## Solution section (end of room)
- **Join a Vihar Seva group** — volunteers who walk/drive along vihar routes with
  reflective jackets, torches, first-aid, and manage traffic around maharaj sahebs
- Display a **vihar-sevak kit**: reflective jacket, lathi, torch, first-aid box, radium
  stickers — makes "seva" concrete, not abstract
- **QR code** on the wall → WhatsApp group / signup form of your local vihar seva mandal
- **Pledge wall**: visitors sign — "Highway par sadhu-sadhvi dekhaay to speed dhimi,
  horn nahi, ane ek phone vihar seva group ne."
- **Raksha mantra** recitation point (as planned)
- Extra idea: a board of **upays** the sangh-level world is pushing — vihar dhams every
  ~15 km on vihar routes, radium danda covers, sanghs informing next village before vihar
