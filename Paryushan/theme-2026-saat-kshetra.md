# Theme 2026 — Saat Kshetra (+ Ashta Karma)

Kalpsutra arrival, day 3 of Paryushan (~10 Sep 2026), SMJV VVN.

## The theme in one line

The seven kshetras are the seven "fields" where a Jain sows their dravya and seva to
earn punya — and the reason we do any of it is to cut the **8 karmas** binding the soul.
That last sentence is the bridge that justifies adding 8 Karma to a Saat Kshetra theme:
**kshetras are the means, karma-kshay is the goal.** Say this at the entrance and the
"8 karma is not a kshetra" objection becomes the theme's punchline instead of a flaw.

---

## 1. Saat Kshetra — the seven fields

| #   | Kshetra                   | What it is                 | Seva / where the dravya goes                                    |
| --- | ------------------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Jin Pratima (Jinbimb)** | Idols of the Tirthankars   | Making, lepa, aangi, kesar-chandan, ornaments                   |
| 2   | **Jin Mandir**            | The derasar itself         | Construction, jirnoddhar (restoration), upkeep                  |
| 3   | **Jin Aagam (Gnan)**      | The scriptures / knowledge | Writing, printing, preserving granths, gnan bhandars, pathshala |
| 4   | **Sadhu**                 | Monks                      | Gochari, vastra, patra, medicine, vaiyavachch                   |
| 5   | **Sadhvi**                | Nuns                       | Same seva as sadhus                                             |
| 6   | **Shravak**               | Laymen                     | Sadharmik bhakti — helping fellow Jains in difficulty           |
| 7   | **Shravika**              | Laywomen                   | Same — this fund is called Sadharan / Sadharmik dravya          |

Key rules worth telling the audience:

- Dravya of a **higher kshetra can never be used for a lower one** (Dev-dravya can't fund shravak welfare), but lower can flow upward.
- Sadharmik bhakti (kshetra 6–7) is repeatedly called the hardest and highest — serving a fellow shravak is serving the shasan's living future.
- Your room grouping: **Sadhu/Sadhvi (one room)**, **Jinbimb + Jinmandir (one)**, **Jin Aagam (one)**, and Mahapurush/Mahasati effectively represents the **Shravak/Shravika kshetra** — ideal shravaks and shravikas of history. Say that explicitly on the room board: it completes all 7 kshetras and answers "where are shravak-shravika?"

## 2. Ashta Karma — the added room

| #   | Karma           | Hides / decides          | Classic analogy                                              | Type   |
| --- | --------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------ |
| 1   | Jnanavarniya    | Knowledge                | **Blindfold** over the eyes                                  | Ghati  |
| 2   | Darshanavarniya | Perception               | **Gatekeeper** blocking the king's darshan                   | Ghati  |
| 3   | Vedaniya        | Pleasure–pain            | **Honey on a sword's edge**                                  | Aghati |
| 4   | Mohaniya        | Right faith & conduct    | **Liquor** — deludes; the most dangerous, root of all kashay | Ghati  |
| 5   | Ayushya         | Lifespan                 | **Jail term** — fixed release date                           | Aghati |
| 6   | Naam            | Body, form, gati         | **Painter** painting many forms                              | Aghati |
| 7   | Gotra           | High/low status          | **Potter** making big and small pots                         | Aghati |
| 8   | Antaray         | Power to give/gain/enjoy | **Treasurer** who blocks the king's own donation             | Ghati  |

- 4 **Ghati** (soul-destroying) + 4 **Aghati**. Kevalgyan = the 4 ghati gone; moksha = all 8 gone.
- Decoration idea addition: end the 8-karma room with a **mirror** and the line
  "aa aath par vijay ej Paryushan" so it loops back to the theme.

## 3. Jin Aagam room — the undecided one (proposal)

**The local hook nobody else has:** the 45 Aagams were written down at the Vallabhi
council under **Devardhigani Kshamashraman** (~Vira Samvat 980) at **Vallabhipur, Gujarat**
— and you are in **Vallabh Vidyanagar**. "Vallabhi thi Vidyanagar" is a ready-made
room title. Bonus tie-in: the **Kalpsutra you just welcomed is itself part of aagam
literature** (by Bhadrabahu Swami, linked to the Chhed sutras) — the room explains the
very granth that arrived today.

**Content — the 45 Aagam tree (Shwetambar Murtipujak count):**

- **11 Anga** (12th, Drishtivad, lost) — Acharang, Sutrakritang, Sthanang, Samavayang, Bhagavati, Gnatadharmakatha, Upasakdasha, Antakruddasha, Anuttaraupapatik, Prashnavyakaran, Vipak
- **12 Upanga** — Aupapatik, Rajprashniya, Jivabhigam, Pragnapana, Surya Pragnapti, Jambudvip Pragnapti, Chandra Pragnapti, Nirayavalika group, etc.
- **6 Chhed sutra** — conduct of sadhu-sadhvi (Kalpsutra's parent Dashashrutaskandha is here)
- **4 Mul sutra** — Dashvaikalik, Uttaradhyayan, Avashyak, Oghaniryukti/Pindaniryukti
- **10 Payanna** + **2 Chulika** (Nandisutra, Anuyogdwar)

**Decoration execution:**

- Big **aagam-vruksh** (tree) on the main wall — trunk = Mahavir's vani, 45 branches
- **Palm-leaf manuscript** replicas (brown paper, tea-stained, Ardhamagadhi lines)
- A scribe's corner: low desk, **boru-kalam, ink, loose folios** — how aagams were hand-copied
- Mini **Aagam Mandir** wall (like Palitana's, where aagams are carved in marble)
- Timeline rope: Mahavir's deshna → Ganadhars → ~1000 yrs oral tradition → Vallabhi lekhan → print → apps
- Audio loop of a Bhagavati Sutra / Uttaradhyayan gatha recitation

## 4. Mahapurush–Mahasati — source: Bharhesar ni Sajjhay

Recited every morning in Rai Pratikraman: **53 mahapurush + 47 mahasati** — reciting
their names at dawn "destroys the fetters of sin." Perfect authority for your room:
the audience already recites these names; your rooms show the _stories behind them_.

### The 53 Mahapurush (sajjhay order, with story hook)

1. **Bharat Chakravarti** — kevalgyan in the mirror-hall (Arisa Bhuvan) from one fallen ring; ruled six khands, attached to none
2. **Bahubali** — one year kayotsarg; pride fell when sisters said "veera, gajthaki utro"
3. **Abhaykumar** — Shrenik's minister-son, sharpest intellect (autpattiki buddhi); chose diksha over the throne
4. **Dhandhankumar** — Krishna's son; vowed to eat only alms got by his _own_ labh; six months without — antaray karma burned
5. **Shriyak** — Sthulibhadra's brother; died in his very first Samvatsari upvas, in samadhi
6. **Arnikaputra Acharya** — kevalgyan mid-river amid upsarg on the Ganga
7. **Atimuktak (Aymatta Muni)** — child muni; floated his patra as a boat, repented so deeply he got kevalgyan
8. **Nagdatta** — forbearance under false accusation _(verify version before scripting)_
9. **Metaraj Muni** — stayed silent as his head was bound with wet leather, to save one bird's life
10. **Sthulibhadra** — 12 years in Kosha's mansion as her lover; one chaturmas there as a muni, unmoved
11. **Vajraswami** — absorbed aagams as an infant hearing sadhvis recite; flew the sangh out of famine
12. **Nandishen** — fallen muni who still converted 10 souls a day; the day the 10th refused, he became the 10th
13. **Arya Sinhgiri** — Vajraswami's guru, farsighted teacher
14. **Krutpunya (Kayvanna)** — one bhav-purna kheer-dan, lifetimes of fortune
15. **Sukoshal Muni** — mauled by a tigress (his own mother reborn); kevalgyan in equanimity
16. **Pundarik** — of the Pundarik–Kundarik brothers; one day of pure bhav outweighed 1000 years of hollow sadhana
17. **Keshi Ganadhar** — Parshva-tradition acharya; the Keshi–Gautam dialogue; converted King Pradeshi
18. **Karakandu** — Pratyekbuddha; enlightened watching an old, once-mighty bull
19. **Halla** and 20. **Vihalla** — renounced after their beloved elephant Sechanak chose death in a fire-trench
20. **Sudarshan Sheth** — framed by a queen; the execution stake turned into a throne by his shiel
21. **Shal** and 23. **Mahashal** — royal brothers; kevalgyan on the road while walking with Gautam Swami
22. **Shalibhadra** — 99 divine boxes daily; one sentence — "there is a master above you" — ended it all
23. **Bhadrabahu Swami** — last Shrutkevali, **author of the Kalpsutra** (say this line aloud in the room — it links to today's event)
24. **Dasharnabhadra** — welcomed Mahavir with the grandest procession; Indra topped it; he topped Indra with instant diksha
25. **Prasannachandra Rajarshi** — ★ see Room plan below
26. **Yashobhadra Suri** — pattadhar in Shayyambhav Swami's lineage
27. **Jambuswami** — converted 8 brides and 500 thieves on his wedding night; last Kevali of this era
28. **Vankachul** — bandit saved again and again by four small rules kept perfectly
29. **Gajsukumal** — burning coals on his head in the smashan; "my father-in-law tied me the turban of moksha"
30. **Avantisukumal** — heard one description of Nalinigulm viman, remembered his past life, gave everything up that night
31. **Dhanna Anagar** — Mahavir himself named him supreme among 14,000 munis in tap
32. **Ilachikumar** — kevalgyan **on the tightrope**, watching a muni refuse what he himself was chasing
33. **Chilatiputra** — dacoit with a severed head in hand; three words — _upsham, vivek, samvar_ — and he stood still while ants ate him for 2.5 days
34. **Bahu Muni** _(reading varies across versions — verify)_
35. **Arya Mahagiri** — revived the severest ascetic conduct
36. **Arya Rakshit Suri** — organized the aagams into the four anuyogas (aagam-room crossover!)
37. **Arya Suhasti Suri** — one diksha to a beggar became Emperor Samprati next birth
38. **Udayan Rajarshi** — the last king to take diksha; Mahavir walked to Vitbhay for him; forgave his poisoner
39. **Manak Muni** — six months to live; the **Dashvaikalik Sutra** was composed for this one child
40. **Kalikacharya (Kalak Suri)** — the acharya because of whom **Samvatsari is on Chauth instead of Pancham** — high awareness value, audience won't know this
41. **Samba** — Krishna's son, renounced
42. **Pradyumna** — Krishna's son, attained moksha
43. **Muldev** — punya of supatra-dan made a wanderer a king _(verify version)_
44. **Prabhav Swami** — came to rob Jambuswami with 500 thieves, left as his first disciple; became Yugpradhan
45. **Vishnukumar Muni** — ★ see Room plan below
46. **Ardrakumar** — prince of a non-Arya land; one Jin-pratima sent by Abhaykumar unlocked his past-life memory
47. **Dridhaprahari** — four murders in one day → diksha at that city's gate → six months of stones and abuse → kevalgyan
48. **Shreyans Kumar** — gave the **first supatra-dan** (ikshu-ras to Rushabhdev) — the origin of Akshay Tritiya and varsitap parna
49. **Kurgadu Muni** — could never fast, ate even on Samvatsari with perfect bhav while tapasvis scorned him — kevalgyan came to him first. _The_ Paryushan story: bhav over kriya
50. **Shayyambhav Swami** — brahmin turned acharya by one line ("the truth is not known, alas!"); composed Dashvaikalik for his son Manak
51. **Megh Kumar** — ready to quit after one bad night on the floor; Mahavir reminded him of the hare he had saved as an elephant

## 5. The three rooms — plan

### Room 1 (locked): Sitaji — "Ramayan you know, Ramayan we hold"

Everyone knows the name; almost nobody in the audience knows the Jain version
(Vimalsuri's **Paumachariya**, the oldest surviving Ram-katha epic besides Valmiki's).
This contrast IS the room — wall table:

| Point                | Common (Valmiki) version         | Jain (Paumachariya) version                                                       |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Who kills Ravan      | Ram                              | **Lakshman** (Ram is the non-violent Baldev; Lakshman the Vasudev)                |
| Ram is               | God-avatar                       | A supremely devout human — the 8th Baldev                                         |
| Ravan is             | Villain-demon                    | A just, handsome Jain king, **protector of Jin shrines**, with one weakness: Sita |
| Agni-pariksha ending | Fire ordeal; earth swallows Sita | **Sita renounces and becomes a Jain sadhvi**, attains devlok                      |
| Sita's birth         | Found in a furrow                | Daughter of Janak and Videha, with a **twin brother, Bhamandala**                 |
| Fate of Ram          | Returns as god                   | **Ram attains moksha as a Jain muni**                                             |
| Monkeys/demons       | Magical beasts and rakshasas     | **Vidyadhar humans** whose clan-banners bore monkey and other emblems             |

Execution: split the room down the middle — familiar imagery on one side, the Jain
telling on the other; end at Sita in white as **Sadhvi**, with the line
"agni ma nahi — sanyam ma gai" (not into the fire — into restraint).

### Room 2 (recommended): Vishnukumar Muni — the same trick, on a "Vaman" story

The audience knows the Vaman avatar's "three steps of land." The shasan has its own
three-steps story, and it's **in the sajjhay**: minister **Namuchi**, owed a boon,
orders the Jain sadhus out of the kingdom; muni **Vishnukumar**, master of labdhi,
asks him for just **three steps of land** — then expands to colossal form, one foot on
each end of the land, and the third step lands on Namuchi. The sangh is saved.
Message: labdhi used _once_, only for sangh-raksha — followed by prayashchit for the
anger. Same "you know this story — now hear ours" energy as the Sita room.

### Room 3 (recommended): Prasannachandra Rajarshi — the bridge to your 8-Karma section

Muni in kayotsarg; hears passing soldiers say his young son is under attack. **In his
mind alone** he fights a full war. Shrenik asks Mahavir: "if he died this instant?" —
"**7th narak.**" Minutes later the muni reaches for a sword, touches his shaven head,
wakes up, repents — "and now?" — "**Sarvarthasiddha.**" Then the devdundubhi:
**kevalgyan.** Same body, same posture, zero outward action — his gati swung from 7th
hell to the highest heaven purely on **bhav**, i.e., on karma-bandh happening in real
time. This room is the 8-karma room's living proof.
Execution: split lighting — red battle-shadow side vs white side; a "gati-meter"
needle that swings as the narration plays.

**Alternate (lighter option for the second mahapurush):** Shreyans Kumar — "you all
know Akshay Tritiya; do you know it is ours?" First supatra-dan (ikshu-ras) to
Rushabhdev after his year-long fast — the origin of varsitap parna. Same awareness
formula, easier set (sugarcane-juice kalash scene).
