# Kalpsutra Arrival — SMJV VVN

## The institution

- **SMJV** — Shri Mahavira Jaina Vidyalaya, founded 1914–15 by Yugdrashta Acharya Shri Vijay Vallabhsuriswarji Maharaj Saheb
- **VVN branch** — Shri Laherchand Uttamchand Vidyarthigruh, Near Nagar Palika, Nana Bazar, Vallabh Vidyanagar (Anand, Gujarat)
- Two units: **Boys Hostel** and **KC (Kanya Chhatralaya / Girls Hostel)**
- Instagram handle: `smjv_vvn`

## The tradition (confirmed)

- The Kalpsutra arrives on the **3rd day of Paryushan** at the Boys Hostel — *"On third day, labh of Kalpasutra Granth was taken by students and sobha yatra of Kalpasutra was held."*
- The **labh** (privilege of bringing the granth) is taken **by the students themselves**, not by the trust or a donor family
- A **sobha yatra / varghoda** escorts the Kalpsutra into the hostel
- At the KC (girls) unit the Kalpsutra has been brought on the **6th day**, welcomed by local committee members
- A **theme with full decoration** is built around the arrival — this is the signature element and has run for **24+ consecutive years**
- The theme is followed by a **grand Bhavna**
- Students also stage a **skit / play** tied to the theme
- **Ghodiya Parnu** is organised during Paryushan
- Students run **Aangi**, **Gahuli**, **Sandhya Bhakti**, morning and evening **Pratikraman**, **Puja-Seva** and **Tap Aradhana** themselves — described as a "self-led" environment
- **Parna** ceremony on the closing day for the tapasvis, attended by parents, local committee and sangh members

## Documented themes

| Edition | Theme | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 24th | **Aho Jinshashanam** | Depicted the past, present and future of Jainism |

**Aho Jinshashanam scale:**
- **500+** visitors from Vallabh Vidyanagar Jain Sangh and outside came to see the theme and decoration
- **200+** people watched the accompanying skit, including Girls MJV and VVN Jain Sangh members
- Students built and executed the entire thing in **under 48 hours**

## Recorded editions

**2018 (6–13 September)**
- Boys: Kalpsutra labh + sobha yatra on day 3; play on **"Tirth Bachavo"**; 29 students in tapasya across 30, 16, 11, 9 and 8 upvas; 80 parents at Tapasvi Parna
- KC: Kalpsutra brought on day 6; 1 student 9 upvas, 2 students 8 upvas, 2 attham; Sanjee and Mehdi ceremonies on Samvatsari

## Typical shape of the Kalpsutra day

1. Labh taken by students
2. Sobha yatra / varghoda through VVN
3. Themed set + decoration revealed
4. Skit / play on the theme
5. Grand Bhavna
6. Open viewing for VVN Jain Sangh and public
