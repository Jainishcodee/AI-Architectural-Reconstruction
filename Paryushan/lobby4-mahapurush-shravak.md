## Section 2 — Maha-Shravaks of history (verified hooks)

### રાજા કુમારપાળ (12th cent., Patan)
- Turned to Jainism by **Kalikal Sarvagna Hemchandracharya**
- **અમારિ ઘોષણા** — ban on animal slaughter across his 14 rajyas; jivdaya at state scale
- Sadharmik bhakti: one humble offering from a **poor shravak** moved him to spend
  crores yearly on fellow shravaks — the model for kshetra 6–7
- Built/restored 1400+ jinalayas; a raja who did Paryushan-level aradhana on the throne

### વસ્તુપાળ – તેજપાળ (13th cent., ministers of Dholka!)
- **Local hook: they were ministers of DHOLKA — barely 30 km from VVN**
- Built the Delwada (Luna Vasahi, Abu) and Girnar jinalayas — marble poetry
- Led massive chha-ri palit sanghs to Shatrunjay–Girnar; legendary "ધર્મના ભંડાર" —
  wealth buried at their death was found spent on shasan instead

### જગડુશા શેઠ (13th cent., Bhadreshwar, Kutch)
- The 3-year famine (VS 1313–15): **opened his granaries and fed lakhs free** — Hindu,
  Muslim, all kingdoms; kings of Gujarat, Sindh and Delhi took grain from him
- "જગતનો પાલનહાર જગડુશા" — a vaniyo who out-fed emperors
- Room line: દુકાળમાં રાજાઓ ઝૂક્યા, એક શ્રાવક સામે.

*(Optional extras if you need a 4th board: Pethadsha, Bhama Shah, Motisha Sheth,
Narshi Natha.)*

## The Kartavyas — "how a shravak/shravika should become" (the room's spine)

Preached every year in the **Ashtahnika vyakhyans before Kalpsutra vanchan** — so
quoting them in THIS room, on Kalpsutra-arrival day, is perfect timing.

### 5 Kartavyas of Paryushan (verified)
1. **અમારિ પ્રવર્તન** — jivdaya, stopping himsa (→ Raja Kumarpal's board! અમારિ ઘોષણા)
2. **સાધર્મિક ભક્તિ** — love/service of fellow shravaks (→ Kumarpal's poor-shravak story; today's Kumarpalbhai board)
3. **ક્ષમાપના** — Michhami Dukkadam (→ Samvatsari itself)
4. **અઠ્ઠમ તપ** — 3-day fast (→ your hostel tapasvis)
5. **ચૈત્ય પરિપાટી** — visiting jinalayas in a group (→ links to lobby 2's yatra message!)

**Use this mapping ON the boards** — every historical gem in the room embodied one
kartavya. That's the thread: રત્નો જુદાં, કર્તવ્ય એ જ.
